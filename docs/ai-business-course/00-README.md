# AI for Business with Claude — The Course

**Version:** 1.0 — 2026-09-24
**For:** Kim. A computer-literate business operator who is not a software engineer.
**Goal:** At the end, you run AI in your business like a pro. You set up projects, write skills, connect tools, run agents, and manage a team of agents from your desk or your phone.

## How to use this course

Read one module per session. Each module has the same parts:

1. **Why it matters** — the business reason.
2. **The concepts** — plain language. Short sentences.
3. **How to do it in Claude** — exact steps and prompts.
4. **Worked example** — a real business problem. Often Tsara Tilapia.
5. **Exercise** — 20 to 60 minutes of hands-on work.
6. **Check yourself** — three questions with answers.

Do the exercises. Reading alone does not make a pro. The exercises build on each other. By module 17 you have a working agent team.

## The one idea behind the whole course

> An AI-native business is one where **people manage agents**, **agents read and write to the business**, and **the business gets smarter over time**. — Theo Tabba

Every module serves one of those three lines.

- Modules 01–03 and 10–11: how to **manage agents**.
- Modules 04–09: how agents **read and write to the business**.
- Modules 12–17: how the business **gets smarter over time**.
